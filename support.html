Subscriptions

How do I cancel?

In TIME8, open Settings and tap Manage subscription. You can also go to the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or current period to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the trial or period ends.

How does the free trial work?

The Yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. It’s available once per Apple ID. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the yearly price is charged to your Apple ID.

I paid but Pro isn’t showing

Check you’re signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it. Then, in TIME8, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. You don’t pay again.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments, so ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Using the app

I can’t hear the beeps or voice

In TIME8, open Settings and check Beeps is on. Voice cues are set for each workout, when you set it up. Then check your iPhone’s volume.

The timer isn’t on my lock screen

In TIME8, open Settings and turn on Live Activity. Then, in the iPhone Settings app, open TIME8 and allow Live Activities.

Why can’t I film my sets?

Filming needs iOS 18 or later. On older versions the rest of TIME8 works as normal, and Pro includes the workout builder. If you have Pro, filming appears once you update to iOS 18, with nothing new to buy.

The camera won’t open, or clips aren’t saved to Photos

In the iPhone Settings app, open TIME8 and allow Camera and Photos. Clips also stay inside TIME8, with the workout you filmed them in.

Why don’t my clips have sound?

Clips are silent on purpose. Recording sound would interrupt your music and the timer’s cues during a workout.

How do I move my workouts to a new iPhone?

TIME8 has no account, so your data lives on your iPhone. It moves with a normal iPhone transfer or restore from an iCloud or computer backup. If you delete the app with no backup, your workouts and history can’t be recovered.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. TIME8 is on iPhone for now.

Training safely

TIME8 is a timer and doesn’t give medical advice. Train within your limits, and speak to a doctor before starting a new exercise programme if you have any health concerns.
