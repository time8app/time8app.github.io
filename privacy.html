Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

TIME8 doesn’t collect your data. There’s no account to create and no server for your data to go to. Your workouts, history and clips stay on your iPhone. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who makes TIME8

TIME8 is made by Asaf Yosef Biton, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. You can reach me at time8app@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

TIME8 stores these on your iPhone. I can’t see them:

  • the workouts you create and save;
  • your workout history, including times, splits, rounds and personal bests;
  • the clips you film;
  • your settings.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, this data is included. I can’t access those backups.

Permissions the app asks for

Camera

Used only when you film a set or open Test filming, which records nothing. Clips are recorded on your iPhone and are silent: TIME8 doesn’t ask for microphone access.

Photos (add only)

Lets TIME8 save your clips to Photos. This permission only adds videos. TIME8 can’t see anything else in your library. You can turn saving off in the app’s Settings, or refuse the permission and keep your clips inside the app.

You can change either permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Subscriptions and purchases

TIME8 Pro is sold through the App Store, and Apple handles the payment. I don’t receive your name, email address or payment details. The app asks Apple, on your iPhone, whether Pro is active. Apple’s handling of your information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Information Apple shares with developers

App Store Connect gives me combined figures such as downloads, sales and product page views. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may also give me crash reports and usage statistics. TIME8 adds no tracking of its own, and Apple doesn’t tell me who you are.

When you share something

TIME8 sends nothing by itself. If you share a workout link, QR code, summary or clip, it goes through the iPhone share sheet to the person or app you pick, and that app’s own privacy terms apply. A workout link contains only the workout itself.

If you contact me

If you email for support, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply and to fix the problem you reported. The legal basis is legitimate interest: I need your message to help you. I keep it only as long as that takes, and I don’t share it with anyone except Google, which runs the mailbox.

Deleting your data

  • History: delete single sessions in the app, or use Delete all history in Settings. Clips kept with a session are deleted with it. Your saved workouts stay.
  • Everything in the app: delete TIME8 from your iPhone.
  • Clips saved to Photos stay in Photos until you delete them there.
  • Support emails: ask me and I’ll delete them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask for a copy of the personal data I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. In practice, the only personal data I could hold is a support email. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

TIME8 isn’t aimed at children under 13. The app doesn’t collect personal data from anyone, whatever their age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date. If TIME8 ever adds something that collects data, such as accounts, this policy will say so before that feature is released.
